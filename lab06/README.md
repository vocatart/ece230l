## Lab Questions

### 1 - How might you add more than two bits together?

For adding more than two bits, you chain multiple adders together that use the carry out from the previous adder.

### 2 - What is the importance of the XOR gate in an adder?

The XOR gate in an adder lets you simplify your logic by handling the carryin for the adder. etc. Y = A ^ B ^ Cin

### 3 - What is the largest number a two bit adder can handle? What happens when you go over?

The largest number a two bit adder can handle is 3 (numbers from 00 -> 11 in binary, hence the name "two bit adder"). Anything more will cause an overflow.
